# Vrinda Store Annual Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Vrinda Store Annual Sales Analysis is an Excel-based data analytics project focused on analyzing **10,000+ sales records** to understand customer purchasing behavior, sales performance, and channel-wise trends.

The project uses **Advanced Excel, Pivot Tables, Charts, and an Interactive Dashboard** to convert raw sales data into actionable business insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Vrinda Store had a large volume of sales data, but raw transaction records made it difficult to identify:

* Which customer segments contribute the most to sales?
* Which age groups and genders show higher purchasing activity?
* Which states generate the highest sales?
* Which online sales channels perform better?
* How can marketing efforts be targeted toward high-contributing customers?

The objective was to analyze the data and identify patterns that could support better sales and marketing decisions.

---

## 💡 Solution

I analyzed **10,000+ sales records** using Advanced Excel and developed an interactive sales dashboard.

### Key Analysis Performed

* Cleaned and analyzed raw sales data.
* Used **Pivot Tables** for customer and sales segmentation.
* Analyzed sales by **gender, age group, state, and sales channel**.
* Created **Charts and Dashboard visualizations** to identify trends.
* Compared performance across major online sales channels.
* Identified high-contributing customer segments and markets.
* Converted analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.

---

## 📊 Key Insights

* **Women customers** contributed a higher share of purchases.
* Customers in the **30–49 age group contributed approximately 50%** of sales.
* **Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh** were identified as key-performing states.
* **Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra** were major contributing sales channels.
* Customer and channel-level analysis helped identify segments with higher sales contribution.

---

## 🚀 Business Impact

The analysis helped translate raw sales data into actionable insights that can support:

* **Targeted Marketing:** Focus campaigns on high-contributing customer segments.
* **Customer Segmentation:** Prioritize the 30–49 age group and other high-value segments.
* **Regional Strategy:** Focus marketing efforts on top-performing states.
* **Channel Optimization:** Evaluate and prioritize major online sales channels.
* **Promotional Planning:** Use targeted offers, advertisements, and coupons for relevant customer segments.

Overall, the dashboard provides a **data-driven approach to understanding sales performance and supporting marketing decisions.**
